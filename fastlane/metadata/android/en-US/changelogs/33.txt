On Android 9 and later, a PDF now opens on a phone whose WebView is too old for PDFs and can't be updated: Android's own renderer draws the pages, walled off from your files, without search or text selection. PDF thumbnails are drawn walled off too. Books, text files and Markdown in Arabic, Hebrew or another right-to-left script read right to left. A slide with no background is drawn on white, as PowerPoint does, and a Word table set to run left to right reads across.
