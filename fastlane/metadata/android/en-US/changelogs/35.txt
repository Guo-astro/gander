Word files saved by WPS Office, LibreOffice or Google Docs now open as pages, with the page readout and Go to page, where Gander showed one long sheet. A Word file with a footer prints one sheet per page, not two. Tabs in a Word file reach their stops, footnotes are numbered through the document, and a file not set for different odd and even pages shows one header and footer on every page. A Graphviz graph saved as .dot or .gv opens as text.
