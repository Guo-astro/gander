EPUB books open in a reader of Gander's own, with the book's contents, a text size, search across the whole book, and the line you left off. A file of an unknown format opens as text when it is text. A double tap zooms a PDF, a Word page, a slide or a picture to twice its width, and the next one puts it back. Gander is offered in Open with and the share sheet for .xlsm, .xlsb, .js, .rb and .sql files.
