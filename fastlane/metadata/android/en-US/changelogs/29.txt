Animated WebP and PNG pictures play, as GIFs do. The title bar scrolls out of sight as you scroll down a document, and comes back when you scroll up or reach the top. A presentation's bullets show as the arrows, squares and ticks they are, where some were empty boxes or the letter l, and the text after them lines up as it does in PowerPoint. Symbols set in Wingdings 2, Wingdings 3 or Webdings show correctly in .doc, .odt and .rtf files.
